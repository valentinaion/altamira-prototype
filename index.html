Readiness scoreSynthetic 0–100 composite: technical 25%, institutional 20%, safeguards 15%, financing 20%, market/revenue 10%, data quality 10%.
NPV proxyIllustrative nominal USD result using a 10% discount rate and simplified stabilized cash-flow assumptions. It excludes tax, FX, detailed depreciation and terminal-value appraisal.
DSCRStabilized operating cash available for debt service divided by annual debt service. Values above 1.0× cover modeled payments; lender requirements may be higher.
Synergy efficiencySynthetic screening proxy: $12M base opportunity plus the sum of four mission weights divided by 18. It estimates shared preparation and enabling costs only.
Integrity scoreSynthetic rules-based composite across identity, duplication, conflicts, feasibility, partner risk and evidence integrity. It prioritizes review and never determines fraud or approval.
